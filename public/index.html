✉A LETTER FROM
THE BEFORE TIMES

It’s 2097. We went back a little further.

Neural implants put Stockfish 99 and 4750 Elo in every head. A few nonchippers missed the days when a game could surprise you. So they formed the Mute Chess Club: a refuge of old computers, unhurried evenings, and bots trained on human games from 2023.

To keep human chess alive, we stopped playing humans.

A note about the fiction and the real project

2097, Stockfish 99, the nonchippers, and the 2023 archive are our fictional setting. The real application uses Maia3 bots trained to predict human moves; this alpha does not claim an exclusively 2023 training set or calibrated ratings. It is an independent, noncommercial, open-source project.
